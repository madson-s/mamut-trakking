# Mamut Trekking

## Product truth

- Mamut is a Chapada Diamantina adventure operator with a direct, local, human voice.
- The site serves travellers researching and booking guided experiences and related logistics.
- This transfer page presents a complementary transport service, separate from trekking packages but within the same brand.
- The primary job is to help a traveller choose an origin and destination, provide date and group details, and request a quote quickly.
- Booking requests are handed off to Mamut through WhatsApp; the website does not take payment in this flow.
- Transfer may be booked as a direct route or combined with a guided stop or adventure.
- Prices shown on the page are starting estimates. Mamut confirms the final amount and pickup details in the conversation.

## Experience requirements

- Reuse the current Mamut header, footer, typography, semantic colour tokens, light/dark theme, focus states and motion language.
- Destination selection is a protected, focused modal flow inspired by the current Vale do Pati mobile booking sheet.
- Mobile is first-class: the planner becomes a bottom sheet with large touch targets and the route/content layout stacks cleanly.
- Static Figma assets are stored locally and rendered with stable geometry and descriptive alternative text where informative.
- Scroll reveals remain subtle, respect reduced motion, and use the site's shared scroll experience.
- The Portuguese route is `/pt/transfer`.

## Content boundaries

- Do not imply live availability, instant confirmation or online payment.
- Do not promise a fixed response time unless the operating team confirms it.
- Treat prices as estimates and label them accordingly.
- Keep WhatsApp as the final conversion step.
